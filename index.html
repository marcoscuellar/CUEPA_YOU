Questions

Fair questions.

I'm not a tech person. Is this for me?

Yes. Most people we work with are beginners. We start wherever you are, even if that means setting up your first AI app together.

What do we actually do in the hour?

We get to know how your life runs, cut through the AI noise, and decide together what AI should take off your plate and what stays yours. Then we set it up and build your dashboard, live, on your own device.

Where do sessions happen?

Over video call. It's virtual, so you can join from anywhere.

Who will I be working with?

Marcos Cuellar and the CUEPA team. Marcos has been learning and studying AI since it came out, and builds AI systems for businesses through CUEPA for Work. You get that same experience, pointed at your everyday life.

How much does it cost?

$129 for one hour of personal AI coaching over video, including your personalized dashboard with the prompts and goals we develop together. Payment confirms your purchase. After you pay, Marcos emails you to confirm your session time.

Do I need ChatGPT or another app first?

No. If you don't have an AI app yet, we'll pick one with you and set it up during your hour. If you already use ChatGPT or another app, we'll build on what you have.

Is this only for business owners?

No. CUEPA for You is for individuals. If you need help for your business or team, that's CUEPA for Work.
